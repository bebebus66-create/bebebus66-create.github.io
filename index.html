AnimeX — anime & short drama, first episode free

This is a small personal notes page about 動漫星球 / AnimeX, a Chinese site that streams anime and vertical short dramas episode by episode. Every title plays per episode and the first one is free, so you can judge a show before paying anything.

The catalogue is large and split into series and short dramas; a typical entry looks like 循环告白 (episode 1), a romance short drama with roughly two-minute episodes.

Notes are updated when a title stands out. Nothing here is sponsored.
